ScanMate — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Applies to the Android app "ScanMate: QR & Barcode Scanner"

Summary

ScanMate is offline-first: scanning, code creation and history work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts and we operate no servers that receive your data.

What the app stores

All of the following is saved only on your phone and never leaves it. Deleting the app removes everything permanently:

  • Scan and creation history (content, type, date)
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, theme, batch mode)
  • Purchase status of the optional Pro upgrade (cached locally)

You can erase your history at any time inside the app: History → Menu → Clear history.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only while the scanner screen is open, to read QR codes and barcodes in real time. No photos or video are recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Vibrate — short confirmation feedback after a successful scan.
  • Internet — used only by the advertising service (below) and when you choose to open a link, share content or send feedback. Scanning and creating codes work completely without it, even in airplane mode.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Google may process your device's advertising ID and device identifiers to serve and measure advertisements, under Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

  • You can opt out of ads personalization in device Settings → Google → Ads → Delete / Reset advertising ID.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (via the UMP SDK) and can choose not to consent to personalized ads.
  • The paid "Pro" version removes all ads entirely.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Google account under Google's policies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Data deletion & your rights

  • History: delete inside the app, or uninstall — all local data is removed.
  • Ad data: reset your advertising ID in device settings (see Advertising above).
  • Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing further for us to export, correct or delete. Requests can still be sent to the contact below.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device and we operate no servers, there is no central database that could be breached. Ads and purchases are handled by Google's infrastructure.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app's release notes on Google Play.
